# A paired ablation of a planted interaction term: held-out AUROC gain, a same-width control and the Bayes-ceiling gap at four signal strengths

## Authors

Author names, affiliations and the corresponding address are to be added by the owner.

## Abstract

We report a paired ablation of an interaction term on a synthetic two-class set whose label is built from the product x3·x4 by design. Four logistic-regression arms were fitted on shared draws for ten seeds at signal strengths 1, 2, 4 and 8. Adding x3·x4 raised held-out AUROC by a mean of 0.0305, 0.0562, 0.0864 and 0.1113, with every 95% bootstrap interval above 0. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#paired_gain} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#paired_gain} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#paired_gain} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#paired_gain} Its margin over the one wrong-pair control tried, x1·x2, also had an interval above 0 at every signal. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#credit_margin} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#credit_margin} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#credit_margin} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#credit_margin} The with-term arm's mean shortfall from the Bayes ceiling fell from 0.0028 at signal 1 to 0.0003 at signal 8. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#ceiling.means} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#ceiling.means} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#ceiling.means} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#ceiling.means} Neither the rule that withholds credit nor the rule that declares a signal failed was triggered, and no generator without the product, correlated proxy or real data set was run, so the report documents what these readings return on a known effect and does not show that they can detect a missing or misattributed one. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#credit_margin} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#credit_margin} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#credit_margin} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#credit_margin} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/report.md#Omnibus} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/e1-checks.json#non_default} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#cells}

## Introduction

An interaction term is the product of two input features, added as a column so that a linear classifier can give it a weight of its own. We ask four questions of the gain such a column brings. How large is it once the variation between draws and fits is taken into account? Is it owed to the right product, or would another extra column of the same width do as well? Must the model be told which pair matters, or does it do as well when given every pair? And at which signal strengths does the gain hold, and how far is the better model from the best ranking the data allow?

We ask them on a generator whose label depends on 1.1·x3·x4 by construction, with a signal strength that scales how strongly the label depends on the features. Four arms are fitted on the same seeded draw for each of ten seeds: the raw features x1 to x4 alone, the raw features plus x3·x4, the raw features plus the wrong pair x1·x2, and the raw features plus all six pairwise products. Each difference between arms is summarized over seeds by a percentile bootstrap interval, with a DeLong interval per seed alongside, and the arm with x3·x4 is compared with the Bayes ceiling, the held-out AUROC of ranking the rows by the generator's own score. The arms, seeds, signal strengths and decision rules were written into a specification before the ablation fits were run, and the headline signal strength was not re-chosen afterwards.

The raw + x3·x4 arm's columns contain every term of the generator's score, and at signal 4.0 it came within 0.0023 AUROC of the Bayes ceiling on every seed, so a positive, credited gain was the expected outcome of this design. {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#ceiling} Every verdict the readings returned was positive, so this report measures the size of the gain, the behavior of two controls and the distance to the ceiling, and does not show that the readings come out negative when an effect is absent or misattributed. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/report.md#Omnibus} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#credit_margin} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#credit_margin} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#credit_margin} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#credit_margin}

The paired gain held at all four signal strengths tried. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/report.md#Omnibus} The smallest signal tried already gave an interval of [0.0259, 0.0355], so the sweep did not locate a signal at which the gain fails. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#paired_gain} At each signal the gain was credited to x3·x4 rather than to the one wrong-pair column tried, x1·x2. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#credit_margin} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#credit_margin} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#credit_margin} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#credit_margin} The all-pairs arm, which is not told which product matters, gave up a mean of at most 0.0021 AUROC relative to the arm that is, though at signal 1 the interval of that cost reached down to -0.0051. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#allpairs_cost} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#allpairs_cost} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#allpairs_cost} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#allpairs_cost} The arm with x3·x4 fell short of the Bayes ceiling by a mean of 0.0028, 0.0015, 0.0009 and 0.0003 AUROC at signals 1, 2, 4 and 8. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#ceiling.means} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#ceiling.means} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#ceiling.means} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#ceiling.means}

## Methods

### Data

Each draw comes from the project's generator, which takes a seed, a signal strength s and a row count. Features x1 to x4 are drawn independently and uniformly on [-1, 1] from one random.Random(seed) stream in row order. A row's latent value is s·f(x) + ε, with f(x) = 0.6·x1 - 0.4·x2 + 1.1·x3·x4 and ε drawn from a standard normal, and its label is 1 with probability equal to the logistic function of the latent value. Each draw has 20,000 rows: the first 16,000 form the training split and the last 4,000 form the held-out split, which is used for scoring only. The sweep uses s = 1, 2, 4 and 8 with seeds 1 to 10, and every arm is fitted on the same 40 draws. The headline signal strength, 4.0, is the generator's default and was fixed before the sweep was run. No external data were used.

### Model and training

Every arm is a logistic regression: a single linear layer over the arm's columns, whose output passed through the logistic function is the row's score. It is trained by minimizing the binary cross-entropy with logits using the Adam optimizer at learning rate 0.1, in minibatches of 256 training rows shuffled each epoch, for 30 epochs, starting from PyTorch's default initialization of the layer under torch.manual_seed(seed). There is no regularization, no early stopping and no convergence check, and nothing was tuned on any split. The four arms differ only in their columns: raw (x1, x2, x3, x4; four columns), raw + x3·x4 (five columns), the wrong-pair control raw + x1·x2 (five columns) and all-pairs (the four raw features and all six pairwise products; ten columns). Pairing is by draw: all arms at a seed and signal share the same rows, while their parameter initializations and minibatch orders, though seeded, are not matched across arms of different width.

### Quantities

Each AUROC is computed on a seed's 4,000 held-out rows as the normalized Mann-Whitney U statistic, with average ranks for ties. For each seed and signal, the paired gain is AUROC(raw + x3·x4) minus AUROC(raw), the credit margin is AUROC(raw + x3·x4) minus AUROC(raw + x1·x2), and the all-pairs cost is AUROC(all-pairs) minus AUROC(raw + x3·x4). Two further differences are tabulated descriptively: AUROC(raw + x1·x2) minus AUROC(raw) and AUROC(all-pairs) minus AUROC(raw).

### Intervals

For each quantity and signal, the interval over seeds is a nominal 95% percentile bootstrap (reference to be added by the owner): 10,000 resamples with replacement of the ten per-seed values, drawn from random.Random(0), with the interval taken as the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles of the resampled means and no small-sample correction. For three ordered pairs of arms, (raw + x3·x4, raw), (raw + x3·x4, raw + x1·x2) and (all-pairs, raw + x3·x4), each seed also gets an interval for the difference in AUROC on its held-out rows by DeLong's method for correlated AUROCs (reference to be added by the owner). With m positive and n negative held-out rows, the placement value of a positive row under an arm is the mean, over negative rows, of a kernel that scores 1, 0.5 or 0 as the positive row's score is greater than, equal to or less than the negative row's, and the placement value of a negative row is defined the other way round. With S10 and S01 the sample covariance matrices of the positive and negative placement values across the two arms a and b, the variance of the difference is (S10(a,a) + S10(b,b) - 2·S10(a,b))/m + (S01(a,a) + S01(b,b) - 2·S01(a,b))/n, and the interval is the difference plus or minus 1.96 times the square root of that variance. The number of seeds with a positive value is reported beside the paired gain's interval.

### Decision rules

The paired gain holds at a signal when the lower bound of its 95% bootstrap interval is above 0 and fails otherwise, so an interval lying wholly below 0 also reads as failing. {res:t5-a1/research/tickets/05-toy-analyze-per-signal-analysis-json-metric-prin.md#notes} Where the gain holds, the credit verdict is true when the credit margin's lower bound is above 0 and false otherwise; where the gain fails, the credit verdict is moot. {res:t5-a1/research/tickets/05-toy-analyze-per-signal-analysis-json-metric-prin.md#notes} Each interval also records a state, above, includes_zero or below, which tells a wholly negative interval from one that includes 0. {res:t5-a1/research/tickets/05-toy-analyze-per-signal-analysis-json-metric-prin.md#notes} Among the intervals reported here, only the all-pairs cost at signal 2 has the state below, and every paired-gain and credit-margin interval has the state above. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#allpairs_cost} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#paired_gain} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#paired_gain} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#paired_gain} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#paired_gain} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#credit_margin} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#credit_margin} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#credit_margin} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#credit_margin} The all-pairs cost and the two descriptive differences carry no verdict, and the paired gain needs no minimum size to hold. The same rules apply at each signal with no correction across signals, and the statement that the gain holds at every signal tried is the conjunction of the four per-signal verdicts. These rules, the arms, the seeds and the signals were written into a specification before the ablation fits were run, and the specification is to be attached by the owner.

### Bayes ceiling

For each seed and signal, the analysis regenerates the held-out rows and computes the AUROC of ranking them by f(x), the score from which the generator draws the label; this is the Bayes ceiling for that draw. It is reported with the raw + x3·x4 arm's shortfall from it and carries no verdict.

### A fixed AUROC target

The project carried a fixed target of 0.82 held-out AUROC, which enters no verdict: the analysis records only how many seeds of each arm reach it at each signal. {res:t5-a1/research/tickets/05-toy-analyze-per-signal-analysis-json-metric-prin.md#notes} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#goal_check.seeds_at_or_above_target}

### Checks on the pipeline

Each (signal, arm, seed) cell has exactly one summary, written once by one training run and never overwritten, and the analysis reads only these summaries and the regenerated held-out rows. The analysis stops without writing any output when a cell is missing, when a summary records settings other than the expected feature set, epochs, learning rate, batch size or row count, or when a summary's held-out labels differ from the regenerated split or between arms. {res:t5-a1/src/toy/analyze.py#AnalysisError} At signal 4.0, all 40 summaries recorded their arm's feature set, 30 epochs, learning rate 0.1, 20,000 rows, batch size 256, the signal, the seed, and 4,000 held-out scores and labels, and the label check passed. {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/e1-checks.json#non_default} Across the sweep the analysis accepted all 160 summaries, ten per arm at each signal, and no cell was re-fitted. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#cells} The trainer's column-selection switch was added for this study, and a fit with no switch and the explicit raw + x3·x4 fit at seed 1 and signal 4.0 gave bitwise-identical held-out AUROC on the same CUDA device. {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/e1-checks.json#reproduction}

### Compute

The per-fit training times recorded in the summaries sum to 641.0 seconds over the four signals, from 160.1 to 160.5 seconds per signal, and the reproduction run recorded 4.0 seconds more. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/report.md#Omnibus} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/e1-checks.json#gpu_seconds} These are the fits' own recorded times, not a measure of GPU occupancy. All analysis ran in standard-library Python on a CPU.

## Results

### The gain at the headline signal

At the headline signal strength of 4.0, the paired gain had a mean of 0.0864 over ten seeds, a 95% bootstrap interval of [0.0829, 0.0897] and a positive value on every seed. {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#paired_gain} Per seed it ranged from 0.0770 to 0.0941. {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#paired_gain} The raw + x3·x4 arm also beat the wrong-pair control: the credit margin had a mean of 0.0862, a 95% bootstrap interval of [0.0827, 0.0897] and a positive value on every seed, the smallest being 0.0774 on seed 2. {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#credit_margin} The wrong-pair column by itself showed no gain over the raw arm that the intervals resolve: the raw + x1·x2 arm minus the raw arm had a mean of 0.00015 and a 95% interval of [-0.00068, 0.00111], which includes 0. {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#wrong_pair_vs_raw} Given all six products and not told which one matters, the all-pairs arm had an all-pairs cost with a mean of -0.00023 and a 95% interval of [-0.00080, 0.00032], which includes 0. {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#allpairs_cost} Relative to the raw arm, the all-pairs arm gained a mean of 0.0862, with a 95% interval of [0.0823, 0.0898]. {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#allpairs_vs_raw}

On every seed the DeLong intervals of the paired gain and the credit margin lay above 0, while the all-pairs cost's DeLong interval included 0 on nine of ten seeds (Fig. 1). {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#paired_gain} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#credit_margin} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#allpairs_cost} The exception was seed 5, where the all-pairs cost was -0.0019 with a DeLong interval of [-0.0036, -0.0003]. {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#allpairs_cost} At this signal the Bayes ceiling ranged from 0.8489 to 0.8637 across seeds, with a mean of 0.8571, and the raw + x3·x4 arm fell short of it on every seed by 0.00004 (seed 5) to 0.0023 (seed 4), a mean of 0.00089. {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#ceiling}

### The fixed AUROC target

At signal 8 the raw arm cleared the 0.82 target on all ten seeds, without the interaction term. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#goal_check.seeds_at_or_above_target} At signals 1 and 2 the mean Bayes ceiling, 0.6224 and 0.7316, lay below 0.82, although the paired gain held at both. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#ceiling.means} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#ceiling.means} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#paired_gain} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#paired_gain} At signal 4.0 the raw + x3·x4 arm's held-out AUROC averaged 0.8562 over the ten seeds, the mean ceiling of 0.8571 less the mean shortfall of 0.00089. {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#ceiling.means}

## Discussion

### What the readings can and cannot show

Every verdict the readings returned was positive: the paired gain held at all four signals and was credited wherever it held. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/report.md#Omnibus} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#credit_margin} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#credit_margin} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#credit_margin} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#credit_margin} Neither the branch that withholds credit nor the branch that declares a signal failed was exercised, so the study shows how the readings behave on a gain that held everywhere, not that they can withhold credit or declare a failure. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#credit_margin} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#credit_margin} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#credit_margin} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#credit_margin} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/report.md#Omnibus} The analysis code's tests include a fixture in which all four arms share identical scores and require the rules to return a failing gain and a moot credit verdict there, and the tests were recorded as passing; this checks the implementation on fabricated scores, not the readings on real fits. {res:t5-a1/research/tickets/05-toy-analyze-per-signal-analysis-json-metric-prin.md#notes} The wrong-pair column is the one added column the label does not use, and its difference from the raw arm had an interval including 0 at all four signals. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#wrong_pair_vs_raw} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#wrong_pair_vs_raw} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#wrong_pair_vs_raw} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#wrong_pair_vs_raw} Read by the paired-gain rule, which the specification did not apply to it, that contrast would fail at every signal, and this reading is post hoc. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#wrong_pair_vs_raw} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#wrong_pair_vs_raw} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#wrong_pair_vs_raw} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#wrong_pair_vs_raw} Every held-out label analyzed came from one generator at signals 1, 2, 4 and 8, and every contrast was among the four arms run, so no generator without the product, no correlated proxy for x3·x4 and no real data set was tested. {res:t5-a1/src/toy/analyze.py#AnalysisError} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/e1-checks.json#non_default} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#cells} The sweep also did not locate where the gain fails, since at the smallest signal tried its interval was already [0.0259, 0.0355]. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#paired_gain}

### The credit test and the missing comparison

In this design the credit test added little: with the wrong-pair difference near 0, the credit margin stayed within 0.0009 of the paired gain at every signal. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#credit_margin} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#credit_margin} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#credit_margin} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#credit_margin} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#paired_gain} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#paired_gain} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#paired_gain} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#paired_gain} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#wrong_pair_vs_raw} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#wrong_pair_vs_raw} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#wrong_pair_vs_raw} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#wrong_pair_vs_raw} The only wrong-pair arm run was x1·x2, so the credit test was never set against a correlated proxy for x3·x4 or, one at a time, against the other four wrong pairs. {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/e1-checks.json#non_default} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#cells} The readings were also not compared with any alternative: every contrast the analysis computed compares held-out AUROC between arms, and no test on the x3·x4 coefficient within the with-term fit, such as a Wald or likelihood-ratio test, was run, so any advantage over such a test is not shown here. {res:t5-a1/src/toy/analyze.py#render_report} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#paired_gain}

### The all-pairs arm

The all-pairs arm asks whether the model must be told which pair matters. Across the four signals the mean all-pairs cost was at most 0.0021 AUROC in size, and its interval included 0 at three of them. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#allpairs_cost} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#allpairs_cost} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#allpairs_cost} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#allpairs_cost} At signal 1, however, the interval reached down to -0.0051 and three seeds lost about 0.008, so at the lowest signal the data do not settle whether the model must be told which pair matters. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#allpairs_cost} We did not vary the number of rows or of features, so how this cost scales with either is not measured here.

### The ceiling and the fixed target

The ceiling measures how much room is left on these features: the raw + x3·x4 arm's mean shortfall from it was 0.0028, 0.0015, 0.0009 and 0.0003 AUROC at signals 1, 2, 4 and 8. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#ceiling.means} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#ceiling.means} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#ceiling.means} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#ceiling.means} Whether an arm cleared the fixed target of 0.82 did not follow the term's contribution: the raw arm cleared it on every seed at signal 8, and at signals 1 and 2 the mean ceiling lay below it while the gain held. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#goal_check.seeds_at_or_above_target} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#ceiling.means} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#ceiling.means} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#paired_gain} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#paired_gain}

### Limitations

Each interval over seeds is a nominal 95% percentile bootstrap from ten values with no small-sample correction, and that caveat applies to every interval reported here; the narrowest exclusion of 0, the all-pairs cost at signal 2 with an upper end of -0.00001, is not read as a cost. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#allpairs_cost} The four per-signal verdicts are uncorrected, and the statement that the gain holds at every signal tried is their conjunction. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/report.md#Omnibus} Every arm is the same logistic regression at fixed defaults with nothing tuned, and we make no claim about other model classes, about models that learn interactions implicitly, or about calibration. The ceiling is defined only where the generating score is known, as it is for simulated data.

### What the report establishes

On one generator, with one model class, ten seeds and four signal strengths, adding x3·x4 gave a held-out AUROC gain that held at every signal and was credited against the one wrong pair tried. {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/report.md#Omnibus} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#credit_margin} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-2.json#credit_margin} {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-4.json#credit_margin} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-8.json#credit_margin} Showing that the readings can withhold credit or declare a failure needs runs this study did not make: a generator without the product, correlated proxies and the other wrong pairs, a comparison with a coefficient test, and real data. {res:t36-a1/analysis/ablation/e1-checks.json#non_default} {res:t37-a1/analysis/ablation/signal-1.json#cells}
