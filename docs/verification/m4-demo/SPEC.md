# Spec: Is UMAP a sound default 2-D embedding for a small branching differentiation time course, and does its picture hold on a day it never saw?

Rendered by `wayline specify` from `research/spec.json`; change the spec by answering its confirmation with what to change, never by editing here.

## Question

On the synthetic time course data/cells.npz (480 cells, 8 days x 60 cells, 50 log-scale genes, one branch point at pseudotime 0.4, made by data/make_cells.py under seed 7), the study asks whether UMAP is a sound default two-dimensional embedding (Q2): whether the picture it draws is Faithful in CONTEXT.md's sense (one branch point, days in order) and whether that picture still holds on the held-out day 7, which nothing may fit, tune or select on. 'Sound default' is read by the Q14 rule: UMAP, at the configuration chosen on the two internal splits (Q6, Q11) and embedding unseen cells by its own transform() (Q15), is sound when its 20-seed interval (Q9) overlaps the best method's on each of the six faithfulness measures (Q8, Q17) on the internal splits and on day 7; when it is not, the measure and the method that beat it are the answer to 'what instead'. The comparison set is the seven methods of Q10, each with its native out-of-sample extension and a common kNN map as a control. The goal, trustworthiness_k15 >= 0.95 on day 7, is reported as one criterion among several and does not stop the study (Q3). Branch-topology controls (Q12), a leave-day-3-out probe of the branch point, and ten replicate datasets (Q5) bound the verdict, whose scope is this generator at the parameters of data/cells.npz only (Q13); the probe is E3, and E3 is conditional on the owner's answer to Q4 (open, human-only): it launches only if the owner takes the probe and is dropped if the owner answers 'it is not', while E1, E2, E4, E5 and E6 launch now.

## Claims

- **C1.** On data/cells.npz, UMAP at the configuration chosen on the internal splits, embedding day 7 by transform(), is a sound default under the Q14 rule: its seed interval overlaps the best method's seed interval on all six faithfulness measures, both on the internal-split average and on day 7 (umap_sound_default = 1). (experiments: E1)
- **C2.** A 2-D embedding fitted on days 0-6 alone reaches the goal: at least one method of the comparison set has a day-7 seed-mean trustworthiness_k15 >= 0.95 under GOAL.json's eval_command (Q3: reported as one criterion among several, not a stop rule). (experiments: E1)
- **C3.** Where UMAP's native-extension interval falls below the best method's on any (measure, condition) of the Q14 rule, its layout is not at fault: under the common kNN map (Q15), which compares layouts alone, UMAP's kNN-control interval on that measure and condition overlaps the best kNN-control interval among the seven methods, so the loss is attributable to transform(), not to the picture. The claim is untested when UMAP loses on no (measure, condition). (experiments: E1)
- **C4.** UMAP's faithfulness on the internal splits depends on its settings, not only its seed: at least one of the four sweep axes of Q11 (n_neighbors, min_dist, init, input PCA dims) has a main effect on at least one of the six faithfulness measures, on the internal-split average, that the stratified permutation test detects at axis_effect_p < 0.05/24 (Bonferroni over 4 axes x 6 measures, so that when no setting matters the claim is supported by chance at most 5% of the time). (experiments: E2)
- **C5.** Initialization governs the global picture more than the local one (the hostile reviewer's objection 7), scored on the same cells and in the same units: init moves the three global measures (day_order_corr, pseudotime_corr and topology_agreement, all computed over training and held-out cells together) by more seed-noise units than it moves the two local measures computed on the same cells (trustworthiness_k15_all and continuity_k15_all). Concretely, init has a detectable main effect on at least one global measure (axis_effect_p < 0.05/24) and the 95% bootstrap interval over the 20 seeds of init_global_minus_local lies wholly above 0. (experiments: E2)
- **C6.** The single branch point is recoverable on cells the embedding never saw: on the leave-day-3-out probe (Q4), at least one method of the comparison set has a native-extension branch_point_placement seed mean no more than 0.1 below the 50-gene reference (B3) on the held-out day-3 cells, i.e. seed mean >= (B3 reference - 0.1); exceeding the reference counts, since B3 is a reference, not a ceiling. (experiments: E3)
- **C7.** UMAP places the branch point out of sample no worse than the best method: on the leave-day-3-out probe, UMAP's (chosen configuration, native extension) seed interval on branch_point_placement overlaps the seed interval of the method with the highest native-extension seed mean among the seven. (experiments: E3)
- **C8.** UMAP does not invent a branch: on the no-branch null control (Q12), once the control's reference check passes (reference_b_components = 1 on D3), invented_branch_rate over 20 seeds is 0 for UMAP at the chosen configuration and at umap-learn defaults, on the layout of days 0-6. (experiments: E4)
- **C9.** The verdict is not an artefact of the generator's noise level: umap_sound_default takes the same value on the noise-sweep datasets at sd 0.3 and sd 1.2 as on sd 0.6 (Q12). (experiments: E5)
- **C10.** The verdict holds across generator seeds: on the ten replicate datasets (Q5), umap_sound_default equals its value on data/cells.npz on at least 8 of 10 (verdict_replicate_count >= 8). (experiments: E6)

## Anti-claims

- **A1.** The verdict is claimed only for data/cells.npz's generator at the parameters of data/cells.npz, seed-replicated by E6 (Q13); it is not claimed for other parameters of the generator. umap_sound_default differing at sd 0.3 or sd 1.2 from its value at sd 0.6 (E5) confirms that the verdict is tied to the parameters it was read at, and the report then narrows the scope to sd 0.6. (confirmed by: E5)
- **A2.** Reaching trustworthiness_k15 >= 0.95 on day 7 is not claimed to show the branching is drawn faithfully (objection 1): any configuration (the seven methods or UMAP at defaults) whose day-7 trustworthiness_k15 seed mean is >= 0.95 under GOAL.json's eval_command while its day-7 native-extension interval on topology_agreement or on day_order_corr lies wholly below the best method's on that measure, with a seed-mean gap of at least 0.05, confirms that the goal's metric and Faithful come apart. study/verdict.py --goal-vs-faithful applies this to every configuration in E1; it is not testable when no configuration reaches 0.95. (confirmed by: E1)
- **A3.** UMAP is not claimed sound if its interval falls below the best method's on any one faithfulness measure in either condition (umap_sound_default = 0 in E1); in that case the report names the measure, the condition and the winning method as 'what instead', and the goal being reached does not rescue the verdict. (confirmed by: E1)
- **A4.** A branch UMAP draws is not claimed to be real: an invented_branch_rate > 0 on the no-branch control (two side-by-side pieces of the trajectory at the same pseudotime, not consecutive clumps), read once the control's reference check has passed, shows that UMAP's picture can contain a branch the data lacks. (confirmed by: E4)
- **A5.** UMAP's default settings are not claimed to be its best: on day 7 in E1, where no selection touched the scores, the defaults grid point's (B2, n_neighbors 15, min_dist 0.1, spectral, 50 dims) native-extension trustworthiness_k15 seed interval may lie wholly below the chosen configuration's day-7 interval; that confirms the defaults are not the best settings for this data. The sweep's internal-split gap between the two is not the test, because the chosen configuration was picked as the best of 108 points on that score and its split interval is inflated by the selection. (confirmed by: E1)
- **A6.** Day 7 is not claimed to test the branch point: it has no pre-branch cells (0/24/36 on branches 0/1/2) and the day-7 arm of the verdict scores only the two branch tips; only the leave-day-3-out probe tests the branch point out of sample. A method's out-of-sample extension is not claimed to preserve the branch point its layout supports: on the probe, a method whose native-extension branch_point_placement interval falls below its own kNN-control interval, with a seed-mean gap of at least 0.05, confirms that its extension, not its layout, misplaces the unseen branch-straddling cells. (confirmed by: E3)
- **A7.** The verdict is not claimed to be seed-independent: verdict_replicate_count < 8 of 10 shows the data/cells.npz verdict is a property of seed 7 rather than of the generator. (confirmed by: E6)
- **A8.** Transfer of the verdict to real scRNA-seq data with thousands of genes, dropout and batch structure is not claimed (objection 2; Q13; CONTEXT.md verdict scope). No experiment in this study touches real data (Q5), so nothing here can test transfer directly; E5 is the experiment whose outcome bears on it, and only in an a fortiori sense: umap_sound_default changing between noise sd 0.3, 0.6 and 1.2 confirms that the verdict does not survive even a change of the generator's own noise model, so it is not portable to data whose noise model (dropout, batch structure, thousands of genes) is unlike the generator's. E5 agreeing across noise levels does not refute this anti-claim; transfer stays unclaimed either way, and the report's verdict names this scope as such. (confirmed by: E5)

## Methods

- **Comparison set and native extensions**: The seven methods of Q10, each fitted on training cells only and placing unseen cells against the frozen fit (Q15): UMAP via umap-learn UMAP.transform(); PCA via projection onto the training components (as eval/pca_sample.py); t-SNE via openTSNE fit then transform() (perplexity 30); diffusion maps (Gaussian kernel, bandwidth = median 15-NN distance, Markov normalisation, two non-trivial eigenvectors) with the Nystrom extension, from pydiffmap or a scipy implementation recorded in the pin file (Q16); PHATE via phate.PHATE(n_components=2).fit then transform(); parametric UMAP via umap-learn's ParametricUMAP if its backend installs under the pins, else a torch MLP (50-64-64-2) trained on UMAP's fuzzy-graph cross-entropy, the choice recorded in the pin file; PAGA-initialized UMAP built through umap-learn UMAP with init = an array of PAGA positions (scanpy PAGA on Leiden groups of the training 15-NN graph in 50-gene space, one position per group plus 0.01 jitter) and otherwise the chosen UMAP configuration, so that it shares plain UMAP's transform(). Competitors other than UMAP run at their library defaults on the 50-gene log-scale data; only UMAP is swept (Q11), which is named as a risk. Every embedding is two-dimensional (Q2). 'The seven methods' always means UMAP at the chosen configuration; UMAP at defaults (B2) is reported beside them and never counts as the best method.
- **Common kNN map control**: For every training layout, each held-out cell is placed at the mean 2-D coordinates of its 15 nearest training cells, neighbours found in the 50-gene log-scale space eval/trustworthiness.py uses, each held-out cell mapped on its own (Q15). k = 15 is fixed and not swept. Because the neighbour sets are identical for every method, the control compares layouts alone. Its scores are reported beside the native extension's per method, measure and condition and read for differences (layout vs extension), never for absolute values and never in the Q14 verdict.
- **Held-out protocol and internal splits**: Day 7 of data/cells.npz is the held-out day: no fitting, tuning or selection touches it, and each final configuration is embedded onto it once per seed and scored (INTAKE.md#Constraints, CONTEXT.md). Every choice of settings uses the two internal splits of Q6 (fit days 0-4, score day 5; fit days 0-5, score day 6) averaged. The same two maps (native, kNN) apply to day 5, day 6, day 7 and the day-3 probe. The selection code reads only cells with day <= 6.
- **UMAP sensitivity sweep and selection rule**: The 108-point grid of Q11: n_neighbors {5,15,30,50} x min_dist {0.0,0.1,0.5} x init {spectral,pca,random} x input PCA dims {10,20,50} (50 dims = no reduction), 20 seeds each, on both internal splits, native transform(). All six measures, plus trustworthiness_k15_all and continuity_k15_all, are recorded at every point and the whole surface is reported. The chosen configuration is the grid point with the highest internal-split average of the seed-mean trustworthiness_k15 (the goal's metric, POSITION.md direction 2 as adopted by Q1), ties broken by the mean of the other five measures; umap-learn's defaults (a grid point) are carried beside it as B2. The selection score is a choice the records leave open and is named as a risk. Whether a setting matters is tested per axis by a stratified permutation test on the full factorial (axis_effect, axis_effect_p), never by comparing pairs of grid points' seed intervals, because a maximum over hundreds of pairwise comparisons exceeds zero by chance alone; the family of 24 (axis, measure) tests is held at a 5% family-wise false-positive rate by a 0.05/24 threshold. Because the grid is balanced, each init level's mean covers the other three axes equally, so init's effect can be read in seed-noise units (init_shift) without the other axes leaking into it.
- **Faithfulness measures**: The six scores of CONTEXT.md, defined under metrics: trustworthiness_k15, continuity_k15 and branch_separability within the held-out cells; pseudotime_corr, day_order_corr and topology_agreement over training and held-out cells together (Q8, Q14, Q17). Two auxiliary scores serve single experiments: trustworthiness_k15_all and continuity_k15_all (the local measures over training and held-out cells together, for C5 in E2) and branch_point_placement (for the day-3 probe, E3). One script, study/measures.py, computes them all from an embedding artifact and a dataset file; its trustworthiness_k15 must reproduce eval/trustworthiness.py to four decimals on eval/sample_pca_day7.npz. Where a measure has a data-side reference (branch_separability, topology_agreement, branch_point_placement), the same quantity computed in 50-gene space is reported as the reference (B3); it is a reference, not a hard ceiling, since a 2-D map that denoises can exceed it, so every comparison to it is one-sided (no more than a stated margin below it).
- **Seed intervals and the verdict rule**: Every stochastic configuration runs 20 embedding seeds, the same seed list on every split and condition; each score is reported as the seed mean with a 95% bootstrap interval (2,000 resamples of the 20 seed values) (Q9); a deterministic method's interval is its point. The internal-split-average condition is formed per seed: for seed s, the score is the mean of its day-5 and day-6 values, and the interval is the bootstrap over the 20 per-seed averages. The verdict (Q14): umap_sound_default = 1 when UMAP's interval overlaps the best method's on every one of the six measures in both conditions (internal-split average, day 7), native extensions only, the best method being the one of the seven with the highest seed mean on that (measure, condition); the report prints every interval and lists each failing (measure, condition, method). The goal's 0.95 is reported beside the verdict, neither necessary nor sufficient for it (Q3).
- **Null controls, noise sweep and replicates**: New scripts beside data/make_cells.py, which stays unchanged (Q5, Q12): data/make_replicates.py (ten seeds, same parameters), data/make_nobranch.py (latent columns 1 and 2 zeroed, branch labels set to 0, same RNG call order), data/make_noise_sweep.py (noise sd 0.3, 0.6, 1.2; the sd 0.6 file must equal data/cells.npz exactly). All write the cells.npz schema (X, day, branch, pseudotime). The no-branch copy is a valid control for invented_branch_rate only when its 50-gene reference shows one piece of trajectory beyond pseudotime 0.6 (reference_b_components = 1), which E4 checks before anything is scored on it.
- **Environment**: A project venv at .venv with exact pins in requirements.lock (umap-learn, scikit-learn, openTSNE, phate, scanpy, torch, and pydiffmap or the scipy alternative), Python and torch build recorded; every study script runs as .venv/bin/python (Q16). The goal's command python3 eval/trustworthiness.py --embedding {artifact} is unchanged.
- **Plot panels**: For each dataset and method (chosen UMAP and defaults both), one PNG panel of the training layout with held-out cells overlaid, coloured by day and by branch, written for the owner to inspect and not scored (Q17).

## Baselines

- **B1.** PCA to two dimensions fitted on days 0-6 and applied to day 7 by projection, trustworthiness_k15 = 0.9206 under GOAL.json's eval_command; the goal's baseline procedure and number, which E1's PCA arm must reproduce. (source: research/GOAL.json#baseline; eval/pca_sample.py; eval/sample_pca_day7.npz)
- **B2.** UMAP at umap-learn's defaults (n_neighbors 15, min_dist 0.1, init spectral, input = all 50 genes, i.e. the grid point 15/0.1/spectral/50) with transform(): the owner's habit, reported beside the chosen configuration in every experiment and never counted as the best method (Q14). (source: Q11, Q14)
- **B3.** The 50-gene reference: branch_separability, the three topology checks (and their component count, reference_b_components) and branch_point_placement computed on the k=15 neighbourhoods of the same cells in 50-gene log-scale space: the data's own neighbourhood structure, which topology_agreement compares against and which is printed beside the other two. It is a reference, not a hard ceiling: a 2-D map that denoises can exceed it, so every rule that uses it is one-sided (no more than a stated margin below it) and never 'within' it. (source: eval/trustworthiness.py's distance space; data/make_cells.py)

## Metrics

- **trustworthiness_k15**: Venna and Kaski trustworthiness at k = 15 within the held-out cells: high-dimensional ranks from their 50-gene log-scale rows, low-dimensional neighbours from their 2-D rows, penalty sum over held-out neighbours ranked > 15 in high dimension, normalised as in eval/trustworthiness.py. The goal's metric on day 7 of D1; on other days and datasets computed by study/measures.py --measure trustworthiness_k15 --held-out-day <d> --data <npz>, which must match eval/trustworthiness.py to four decimals on eval/sample_pca_day7.npz. (script: `python3 eval/trustworthiness.py --embedding {artifact}`)
- **continuity_k15**: The symmetric quantity at k = 15 within the held-out cells: penalty over pairs where j is among i's 15 nearest in 50-gene space but ranked r > 15 in the 2-D embedding, summing r - 15, same normalisation as trustworthiness. (script: `.venv/bin/python study/measures.py --measure continuity_k15 --embedding {artifact} --data {dataset} --held-out-day {d}`)
- **trustworthiness_k15_all**: trustworthiness at k = 15 computed over training and held-out cells together (the whole scored embedding against the same cells' 50-gene rows); used only for C5 in E2 so that the local and global sides of the contrast are scored on the same cells. (script: `.venv/bin/python study/measures.py --measure trustworthiness_k15 --all-cells --embedding {artifact} --data {dataset} --held-out-day {d}`)
- **continuity_k15_all**: continuity at k = 15 computed over training and held-out cells together; used only for C5 in E2. (script: `.venv/bin/python study/measures.py --measure continuity_k15 --all-cells --embedding {artifact} --data {dataset} --held-out-day {d}`)
- **pseudotime_corr**: Spearman correlation, over training and held-out cells together, between true pseudotime and the embedding order: shortest-path distance from the root on the symmetric k=15 kNN graph of the 2-D embedding of those cells with Euclidean edge weights, the root being the training cell with the smallest true pseudotime; cells unreachable from the root get the largest finite distance plus one. (script: `.venv/bin/python study/measures.py --measure pseudotime_corr --embedding {artifact} --data {dataset} --held-out-day {d}`)
- **day_order_corr**: Spearman correlation, over training and held-out cells together, between the same embedding order (geodesic distance from the root as in pseudotime_corr) and the day label; scores the 'same ordering of days' half of Faithful (Q17). (script: `.venv/bin/python study/measures.py --measure day_order_corr --embedding {artifact} --data {dataset} --held-out-day {d}`)
- **branch_separability**: Leave-one-out k=15 nearest-neighbour majority-vote accuracy of the branch label (1 vs 2) in the 2-D embedding among held-out cells with branch in {1, 2}; the same accuracy in 50-gene space is reported as the reference (B3). Undefined on D3. (script: `.venv/bin/python study/measures.py --measure branch_separability --embedding {artifact} --data {dataset} --held-out-day {d}`)
- **topology_agreement**: On the symmetric k=15 kNN graph of the 2-D embedding of training and held-out cells, three binary checks: (a) the graph has one connected component; (b) the subgraph on cells with true pseudotime >= 0.6 has exactly two connected components of at least 10 cells, each at least 90% one branch label and the two majority labels different; (c) the subgraph on cells with pseudotime < 0.4 has one connected component. Agreement = fraction of the three checks whose outcome equals the outcome of the same check on the k=15 kNN graph of the same cells in 50-gene space (B3; on a valid D3 the reference gives one component in check (b), and the check then agrees when the embedding gives one). 1 means the embedding's kNN-graph topology agrees with the single known branch point wherever the data itself shows it. Note: the held-out cells are a minority in every check (with day 7 held out, its 60 cells sit among the roughly 200 cells with pseudotime >= 0.6 in check (b); with day 3 held out, its 18 branch-0 cells sit among the roughly 200 cells with pseudotime < 0.4 in check (c), and no day-3 cell reaches check (b)), so the measure scores the topology of the layout with the held-out cells placed in it, and the placement of held-out cells at the branch point is scored by branch_point_placement. (script: `.venv/bin/python study/measures.py --measure topology_agreement --embedding {artifact} --data {dataset} --held-out-day {d}`)
- **branch_point_placement**: For each held-out cell i, N(i) is the set of its 15 nearest training cells in the 2-D embedding. Per-cell score in {0, 0.5, 1}: 0.5 for stage if the median true pseudotime of N(i) is within 0.1 of i's own true pseudotime (i lands at its own stage, neither back on the trunk's start nor out at an arm tip); 0.5 for arm if, for a branch-1 or branch-2 cell, at least half of the members of N(i) with branch != 0 lie on i's own arm (0 when no member of N(i) lies on an arm), or, for a branch-0 cell, at least half of N(i) has branch 0. The measure is the mean per-cell score over the held-out cells. The same score with N(i) taken in 50-gene space is the reference (B3), one-sided: a 2-D map may exceed it. On the day-3 probe it scores whether the unseen branch-straddling cells are placed at the branch point, on the correct arm, at their stage. (script: `.venv/bin/python study/measures.py --measure branch_point_placement --embedding {artifact} --data {dataset} --held-out-day {d}`)
- **reference_b_components**: On a dataset's cells (its training cells, days 0-6, under --train-only), the number of connected components of at least 10 cells in the symmetric k=15 kNN graph in 50-gene log-scale space restricted to cells with true pseudotime >= 0.6: check (b)'s component count on the 50-gene reference (B3). It is the precondition of invented_branch_rate: the no-branch control D3 is valid only when it equals 1, meaning the data's own neighbourhoods show one piece of trajectory beyond pseudotime 0.6. Reported for every dataset the study scores. (script: `.venv/bin/python study/measures.py --reference-check --data {dataset} --train-only`)
- **invented_branch_rate**: Defined only on a dataset whose reference check has passed (reference_b_components = 1 on the same cells); otherwise recorded as undefined and the control reported invalid. On such a dataset, the fraction of the 20 seeds in which the embedding's k=15 kNN graph on cells with pseudotime >= 0.6 has two or more connected components of at least 10 cells each whose true-pseudotime ranges (5th to 95th percentile within the component) overlap by at least 0.1, i.e. two pieces of the trajectory running side by side at the same stage; consecutive clumps along the trajectory do not count. Computed on the training layout. (script: `.venv/bin/python study/measures.py --measure invented_branch --embedding {artifact} --data {dataset} --train-only`)
- **axis_effect**: For one sweep axis (n_neighbors, min_dist, init or pca_dims) and one measure, on the per-seed internal-split average: the axis's main-effect share of variance, eta-squared = SS_axis / SS_total over the 2,160 runs (108 grid points x 20 seeds), where SS_axis = sum over the axis's levels of (number of runs at the level) x (level mean - grand mean)^2 and SS_total = sum over runs of (value - grand mean)^2. Unitless in [0, 1]. The grid is a full factorial, so each level's mean covers the other three axes equally and SS_axis carries only that axis's effect plus seed noise. It is a size, not a test; its test is axis_effect_p. (script: `.venv/bin/python study/sweep_summary.py --scores results/E2/scores.jsonl --axis {axis} --measure {measure}`)
- **axis_effect_p**: The stratified permutation p-value of axis_effect. A stratum is one combination of the other three axes' levels (27 strata of 4 grid points for n_neighbors; 36 strata of 3 for min_dist, init and pca_dims). Each of 5,000 permutations (numpy default_rng(0)) reassigns the axis's levels among the grid points within every stratum, each grid point's 20 seed values moving with it as a block, and recomputes axis_effect; p = (1 + number of permutations with axis_effect >= the observed value) / 5001. Under the null that the axis's level does not change the measure's distribution, the grid points within a stratum are exchangeable, so the test is exact and the other axes' effects are held fixed. The smallest attainable value, 1/5001, lies below the 0.05/24 threshold that C4 and C5 use, and 24 tests at that threshold hold the family-wise false-positive rate at 5%. (script: `.venv/bin/python study/sweep_summary.py --scores results/E2/scores.jsonl --axis {axis} --measure {measure} --permutations 5000`)
- **init_shift**: For one measure and one seed s, on the internal-split average: the range (largest minus smallest) over the three init levels of the level's mean at seed s (each mean over the 36 grid points at that level), divided by the measure's pooled seed SD (the square root of the mean over the 108 grid points of the sample variance across the 20 seeds at that point, computed once from all runs). Unitless: how far init moves the measure at that seed, in seed-noise units, which puts Spearman correlations, the four-valued topology_agreement and the local measures on one scale. Because the grid is balanced, each level mean covers the same 36 strata, so the other axes' effects cancel and only init's effect and seed noise remain. Undefined when the pooled seed SD is 0; such a measure is dropped from its side of the contrast and the report says so. (script: `.venv/bin/python study/sweep_summary.py --scores results/E2/scores.jsonl --init-shift --measure {measure}`)
- **init_global_minus_local**: Per seed s: the mean of init_shift(s, m) over the three global measures m in {pseudotime_corr, day_order_corr, topology_agreement} minus the mean over the two local measures m in {trustworthiness_k15_all, continuity_k15_all}, all five computed over training and held-out cells together; reported as the mean over the 20 seeds with its 95% bootstrap interval (2,000 resamples of the 20 per-seed values, as Q9's seed intervals). Positive means init moves the global picture more than the local one on the same cells, in seed-noise units; it is read only after init's effect on a global measure is detected by axis_effect_p (C5's guard). (script: `.venv/bin/python study/sweep_summary.py --scores results/E2/scores.jsonl --init-contrast`)
- **umap_sound_default**: The Q14 rule as a number: 1 when, for each of the six faithfulness measures and each of the two conditions (internal-split average, formed per seed as the mean of the day-5 and day-6 scores and bootstrapped over the 20 per-seed averages; day 7), UMAP's seed interval (chosen configuration, native extension) overlaps the seed interval of the one of the seven methods with the highest seed mean on that measure and condition (a deterministic method's interval is its point; UMAP at defaults is never the best method); else 0, with every failing (measure, condition, method) listed. The kNN control and the day-3 probe do not enter it. (script: `.venv/bin/python study/verdict.py --scores results/{experiment}/scores.jsonl --dataset {dataset}`)
- **verdict_replicate_count**: The number of the ten replicate datasets (D2) on which umap_sound_default equals its value on D1. (script: `.venv/bin/python study/verdict.py --replicates results/E6/scores.jsonl --reference results/E1/verdict.json`)

## Risks

- Q4 is open and human-only: the spec takes no side on it. E3 is conditional on the owner's answer to Q4 (open, human-only): the leave-day-3-out probe (E3), with C6, C7, the second clause of A6, branch_point_placement and the branch-point success criterion, waits on that answer and launches only if the owner takes the probe, so that the branch point is tested on unseen cells; if the owner answers 'it is not', E3 is dropped. E1, E2, E4, E5 and E6 do not wait on Q4 and launch now. Mitigation: E3 is separable: it enters no other experiment and not the Q14 verdict, is reported beside it, and never reads day 7; dropping it changes nothing in E1-E2 or E4-E6. branch_point_placement is used by E3 alone.
- The sweep's selection score is not settled by any decision; the spec selects on the internal-split average of trustworthiness_k15 (the goal's metric), which could pick a configuration weak on the global measures. Mitigation: E2 reports the whole surface on all six measures; the defaults (B2) are carried beside the chosen point everywhere; the report also shows the grid point with the best mean rank across the six measures, without switching the verdict to it.
- Choosing the best of 108 points on two 60-cell days is selection-optimistic, so the chosen configuration's internal-split scores overstate day 7 (Q11's named cost), and any comparison against the chosen point on its selection score is inflated. Mitigation: The verdict requires day 7, untouched by selection, as well as the two-split average (Q6, Q14); the sweep's best split value is reported as the reference and the day-7 value beside it; A5 (defaults vs chosen) is decided on E1's day-7 rows only, and the split-side gap is printed as descriptive.
- Interval overlap is lenient per comparison and a conjunction of about twelve comparisons is strict overall (Q14's named costs); one chance non-overlap fails UMAP, wide UMAP spread passes it. Mitigation: Every interval is printed with its width; the failing triples are listed so the owner can see whether a failure is one narrow miss; replicates (E6) show how often the same triple fails.
- Only UMAP is swept while competitors run at library defaults, which favours UMAP: a 'sound' verdict is generous, a 'not sound' verdict is conservative. Mitigation: B2 (UMAP defaults) is reported beside the chosen configuration so the owner can read the verdict at defaults too; the report states the asymmetry; tuning competitors is out of scope.
- Each scored day is 60 cells and k = 15 covers a quarter of it, so every measure is coarse; on the day-3 probe branch 1 vs 2 cells are nearly indistinguishable by construction (s <= 0.13), so branch_separability there is near chance for every method and does not enter the probe's claims. Mitigation: 20 seeds with bootstrap intervals (Q9), two internal splits (Q6), ten replicates (Q5); the probe is scored by branch_point_placement, whose arm half uses the arm-membership of training neighbours from day 4 onward rather than day-3 cells' own separation, and B3's 50-gene reference is printed beside every referenced measure so a low value the data itself does not reach is not read as a method failure.
- The thresholds inside topology_agreement (pseudotime >= 0.6 for tips, < 0.4 for the trunk, 10-cell components, 90% purity), invented_branch_rate (0.1 pseudotime overlap), branch_point_placement (0.1 stage window, majority arm rule), the 0.1 margin below the reference in C6, the 0.05 seed-mean gaps in A2 and A6, and the sweep tests' 5,000 permutations and 0.05/24 threshold are the spec's own choices and could be tuned to a result. Mitigation: They are fixed here before any embedding is scored, apply identically to every method and axis, and the referenced measures are compared against the 50-gene reference so the data's own kNN neighbourhoods set the expectation; the permutation threshold is the plain Bonferroni bound for the 24-test family.
- C5's contrast standardizes each measure by its pooled seed SD, but seed noise is heteroscedastic across grid points (random init spreads more than spectral), so the null expectation of init_shift is not exactly equal across measures and a small spurious difference between the global and local sides is possible when init has little effect. Mitigation: C5's rule reads the contrast only after init's main effect on a global measure is detected by the stratified permutation test (axis_effect_p < 0.05/24), the contrast's interval must lie wholly above 0, and the report prints init_shift per measure with each init level's pooled seed SD so the owner can see how far the sides differ in noise structure.
- The no-branch control D3 may not give one component in the 50-gene reference's check (b) at k = 15: with the branch columns zeroed the latent is a one-dimensional curve under sd-0.6 noise on 50 genes, and its kNN graph beyond pseudotime 0.6 could fragment into pieces of 10 or more cells, in which case invented_branch_rate is undefined. Mitigation: E4 runs the reference check (reference_b_components) first and, if it fails, reports the control invalid and C8 and A4 not testable rather than re-tuning k or the thresholds; the check is also a seam test of data/make_nobranch.py, so the failure is seen when the dataset is built, not when the rate is read.
- Library availability under Q16: pydiffmap may not install, and umap-learn's ParametricUMAP backend may not match the pinned torch. Mitigation: Diffusion maps with Nystrom fall back to a scipy implementation and parametric UMAP to a torch MLP, both named under methods and recorded in requirements.lock.
- The kNN control can only interpolate, so its day-7 placements pile onto day 6 for every method and its absolute day_order_corr and pseudotime_corr are not what a user sees. Mitigation: The control is read only for differences between methods and between a method's native and kNN maps, never enters the verdict, and the report says so (Q15).
