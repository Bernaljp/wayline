# Referee report 1

The manuscript is a pleasure to read: the question is clear, the colonies are well described,
and the figure makes the central result plain at a glance.

1. The abstract says synchrony peaks at dusk, but the Results say the order parameter is lowest
   at dusk. One of the two must be wrong.
2. The size series needs a control: (a) report how many moths were filmed per colony, and
   (b) show that the camera frame rate does not alias the wingbeat.
3. The phase window is not stated consistently between the Methods and the code.
4. The authors should rule out lamp flicker in the enclosure as the cue that drives synchrony.
5. The dusk sweep of 2021 is cited with a value of R but its data are not supplied.
