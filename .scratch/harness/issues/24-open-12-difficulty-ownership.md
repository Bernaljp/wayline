# 24 — OPEN-12 Difficulty ownership

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: None
Brief: OPEN-12, Section 8.4

## Question

The planner assigns difficulty with the rubric in 8.4 and the conductor adjusts from outcomes (default), or the human assigns?

Default: planner plus outcome adjustment. Confirm, and fix the adjustment rule: a kind that fails twice at a tier raises that kind's default by one for the rest of the run; the human can pin a difficulty in the ticket file.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-09-11 by the owner in a batch grilling round (all defaults confirmed unless stated).

The planner assigns difficulty with the 8.4 rubric. After two failures at a tier, the conductor raises that kind's default by one tier for the rest of the run. The human can pin a difficulty in the ticket file.
