# 60 - The rung ladder

**What to build:** A ticket's difficulty names a rung of the rung ladder instead of a model and an effort. `policy.json` carries `tiers`, an ordered list of seven rungs (a `model`, an `effort`, and a `budget`), cheapest first, and `difficulty` becomes a map from each level 1 to 5 to a rung index (2, 3, 5, 6, 7). The planner keeps grading 1 to 5, and every ticket already graded keeps its level. The cap holds: ticket work never runs above `high` effort, so a ticket at difficulty 4 launches on `fable low` and one at difficulty 5 on `fable high`, while `policy.roles` (the planner, decider, writer, and evolver) and every verdict stay where they are. The owner sees each launch as `r5 opus high` in `wayline status` and on the dashboard's ticket page. An existing project whose `harness.json` overrides the old `difficulty` table is refused with a message naming `wayline upgrade`, and `wayline upgrade` rewrites that override to the nearest rungs and prints what it changed. This is the first build ticket of the finer tiers map. The escalation chain, pacing, and the fallback keep their current behavior here and move to rungs in "61 - Escalation by rung" and "62 - Pacing and the fallback by rung".
