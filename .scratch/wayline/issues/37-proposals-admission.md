# 37 - Proposals admission

**What to build:** Workers, verifiers, and the critic may propose tickets, and code decides: a proposal is admitted when it is inside the radius, not a duplicate, under the per-wave cap, and its blockers exist; otherwise it is parked with the reason, listed in the report under "not pursued", never deleted, and reconsidered by the decider at the end of each wave under `autonomous`.

**Blocked by:** 07 (Frontier, launchable subset, and two parallel workers), 31 (The decider)

**Status:** done

**GitHub:** [#38](https://github.com/Bernaljp/wayline/issues/38) (mirror; the local file is the source)

**Milestone:** M5 Overnight and report. **Spec:** Implementation Decisions 5 (proposals), 14 (`proposals`); OPEN-13 (map ticket 25); BRIEF 6.3, 9.5.

- [x] Admission runs on every result's `proposals[]` and on the critic's and verifier's proposals: inside the radius (`strict` admits only tickets tracing to a spec claim; `moderate` adds follow-ups probing a claim's failure; `exploratory` adds new directions gated on a scout novelty check), not a duplicate (same kind and normalized-title similarity above 0.8, or identical touch set and origin, is a duplicate; similarity between 0.6 and 0.8 goes to one `haiku --effort low` judgment through the launcher), under the per-wave cap (`strict` 0, `moderate` 3, `exploratory` 6), every `blocked_by` existing. (`wayline/proposals.py`'s `admit_proposals`; wired into `Conductor._admit_proposals` for a worker's result and `Conductor._admit_verdict_proposals` for a verifier's or the critic's verdict, both `wayline/conductor.py`. The radius check compares a proposal's own declared radius against `harness.json`'s; `strict`'s claim-tracing is enforced structurally by its cap of 0, and `exploratory`'s scout novelty check is the shared duplicate check below, since nothing in the proposal schema names a claim id to trace. The dedup judge is the new `dedup-judge` role, `roles/dedup-judge/`, `schemas/dedup_verdict.json`.)
- [x] Admitted proposals become tickets with `proposed_by` and `origin` and journal `proposal_admitted`; the rest journal `proposal_parked` with `reason` in `radius, duplicate, cap, blockers` and are kept under `research/proposals/` for the report. (Never deleted: an `apply_admitted` admission rewrites a parked file's `status` rather than removing it.)
- [x] Under `autonomous` the decider reconsiders parked proposals at the end of each wave and may admit them within the cap. (Ticket 31's `decider.decide_proposal` already journaled the opinion; `proposals.apply_admitted`, called from `Conductor._reconsider_parked`, reads it back and actually admits an `admit` within the cap.)
- [x] The report renderer lists parked proposals under "not pursued" with their reasons. (Already built by ticket 31/58's report work; `tests/test_report.py`'s `test_parked_proposals_are_listed_as_not_pursued` covers it, unchanged by this ticket.)
- [x] Tests drive the fake worker with proposals across the three radii, duplicates at 0.85 and 0.7 similarity (fake judge), and a cap overflow. (`tests/test_proposals.py`: `RadiusTest`, `DuplicateTest` (mechanical ~0.85, judged ~0.71 through a scripted fake `dedup-judge` session), `CapTest`, `BlockersTest`, `ParkedTest`, `ApplyAdmittedTest`, and `ConductorAdmitsProposalsTest` driving the fake worker through `fixtures/projects/one-ticket/`.)

**Judgment calls, for the reviewer:** `schemas/result.json`'s and `schemas/verdict.json`'s `proposals[]` items gained an optional `touches` field (absent before this ticket) so the "identical touch set" duplicate rule has something to compare; `proposed_by` for a verifier's or the critic's proposal is `verifier` (the ticket schema's `proposed_by` pattern names no `critic:<n>`, only `verifier`, and the critic's depth spot-check is a Type-B verdict like the verifier's own); an admitted proposal's `role` and `difficulty` (1) are code's own default by `kind` (`wayline.proposals.KIND_ROLE`), since nothing else sizes a ticket nobody planned.
