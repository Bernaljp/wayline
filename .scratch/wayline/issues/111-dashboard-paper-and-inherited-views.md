# 111 - Dashboard: the Paper and Inherited views

**What to build:** Two more of the five views, built the way 110 built Research and Run: thin lists with item links, read only in the popup viewer, and working at phone width.

- **Paper** shows:
  - the five sections and the outline;
  - References with its Not yet resolved rows;
  - the code repository with its link, visibility, and last push;
  - Methods' parameter table and the methods with no code;
  - a place for the paper order's card.
- **Inherited** appears only when the project has documents. It shows:
  - the documents and their headings;
  - concerns with their sources and standing;
  - inherited claims with their standing, verdict, and route;
  - unverifiable claims with their reasons;
  - contradicting pairs;
  - the setup's merges;
  - the missing material with the owner's replies.

Not part of this ticket:

- the paper's parts with versions and alternatives, earlier versions, the compilations' downloads, and the order's card itself (121);
- the edit buttons on the sections, the outline, and parameter reasons (113);
- the marks view (112).
