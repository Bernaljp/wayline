# 62 - Pacing and the fallback by rung

**What to build:** A nearly spent window stretches further instead of making hard tickets wait. At `pacing.cheap_at`, every ticket launch runs one rung down, with rung 2 (`sonnet low`) as the floor, and this replaces today's `cheap_only` deferral of tickets above `schedule.CHEAP_MAX`. A Fable rung reads Fable's weekly window and the all-models week; every other rung reads the five-hour and all-models windows. On Fable's own window the step takes a launch from rung 7 to rung 6, and pacing never steps a launch below rung 6 out of Fable, which only the hold and the fallback do. An attempt that is itself a tier up is never stepped down, or pacing would cancel the escalation. The hold still holds Fable rungs alone at `stop_at`, and a held Fable rung falls back to the Opus rung at the same effort and budget (rung 6 to `opus low`, rung 7 to `opus high`), with `fallback_from` naming the rung it came from. While a step down is in force, the window line of `wayline status` and the dashboard says so.
