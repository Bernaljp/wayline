# 04 - Opus 5.5: what `opus` launches now, what it costs, and where it ranks

Written 2026-09-23 at the owner's request, before any of the four open tickets (47, 50, 51, 53) runs; no ticket. It updates [02](02-version-cost.md) and [03](03-version-quality.md) for the Opus version released on 2026-09-22.

- **Claude Code version:** 2.1.280, binary at `/home/bernaljp/.local/share/claude/versions/2.1.280`.
- **Windows at the probe:** five-hour 22%, all-models week 14%. The probe moved neither.
- **Spend of the live probe:** $0.46 of list-price token value across three launches, none on Fable.

## The short answer

1. **`--model opus` now launches `claude-opus-5-5`.** The probe's `system/init` and `modelUsage` both name it, and the CLI's catalog maps the alias there. The harness accepts it: an alias rung matches any version of its family, and `pacing.family("claude-opus-5-5")` is `opus`. A rung that names `claude-opus-5` still launches Opus 5 under `CLAUDE_CODE_DISABLE_LEGACY_MODEL_REMAP=1`, which every worker launch sets.
2. **It costs less than Opus 5 for the same work.** Its price is $4 and $20 per million input and output tokens, $8 for a 1 hour cache write, and $0.20 for a cache read, against Opus 5's $5, $25, $10, and $0.50. It keeps the new tokenizer and the lean prompt, so the matched probe billed nearly the same tokens as Opus 5: **0.72x Opus 5 at `high`**. Fable 5.1, 2.06x Opus 5 in note 02, is about **2.9x Opus 5.5 at `high`**.
3. **It ranks above Fable 5.1 on every row of the system card's summary table**, at max effort. That includes every benchmark on which note 03 ranked Fable 5.1 above Opus 5: SWE-bench Pro, Terminal-Bench 4.0, Terminal-Bench-Science, FrontierSWE v2, CursorBench, and AutomationBench. Most of the lead holds below max. On CursorBench 4.0, Opus 5.5 at `high` (56.0%) and at `medium` (52.5%) both score above Fable 5.1 at `max` (51.8%).
4. **So the two Fable rungs are dominated.** Rungs 6 and 7 now cost more than rung 5, both in dollars and in Fable's weekly window, and score lower on every published benchmark. Decision 16 keeps a rung only when it sits between its neighbors on cost and quality, and it put Fable last because Fable was the strongest model. The same holds for the four roles on Fable (the planner, the decider, the writer, the evolver) and for the verifier of an Opus author.

## 1. The catalog entry, verbatim

From `strings` on the 2.1.280 binary. The `provider_ids` map is elided with `...`.

```
{id:"claude-opus-5-5",family:"opus",display_name:"Opus 5.5",knowledge_cutoff:"June 2026",...,fallback_3p:"claude-opus-5",context:{window:1e6,native_1m:!0,supports_1m_beta:!0,supports_1m_suffix:!0},max_output_tokens:{default:128000,upper:128000},pricing:"tier_4_20_cache_read_0_20",capabilities:["effort","max_effort","xhigh_effort","adaptive_thinking","rejects_disabled_thinking","mid_conv_system","mid_conv_tool_change","per_turn_effort","per_turn_timing","context_management","fast_mode","lean_prompt","refusal_fallback","opus_5_5_prompt_bundle"],default_effort:"medium",image_limits:{maxWidth:2000,maxHeight:2000},advisor_rank:4}

aliases:{opus:{default:"claude-opus-5-5",...},sonnet:{default:"claude-sonnet-5",...},haiku:{default:"claude-haiku-4-5"},fable:{default:"claude-fable-5-1",...}}
```

What it changes:

- **The default effort is `medium`**, where Opus 5's default was `high`. Nothing in wayline inherits the default. Every worker launch passes the rung's or the role's `--effort`, and every launch line in `models.md` names the effort.
- **No `effort_cost_index`.** The effort picker shows no multiplier for Opus 5.5, and the catalog gives no estimate for it at `low`.
- **Refusal fallbacks stay in the family.** The CLI sends a refused `claude-opus-5-5` request to `claude-opus-5` for `bio` and `frontier_llm`, and to `claude-opus-4-8` for `cyber`. An alias rung that falls back still passes ticket 63's check.

## 2. The matched probe

The probe used note 02's method on 2.1.280: the same prompt and the same five files (275, 830, 1200, 2100, and 4500 bytes) in a `mktemp -d` directory under `/tmp`. Each launch took `--max-turns 3 --output-format stream-json --verbose --allowedTools Bash,Glob,Read`, one launch per row. Every launch took 2 turns and answered correctly. 2.1.280 bills no Haiku preflight, so `total_cost_usd` is the main model alone.

| `--model`, `--effort` | Ran (`system/init`) | 1 hour cache write | Cache read | Output | `total_cost_usd` | x Opus 5 at `high` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `claude-opus-5`, `high`, no remap | `claude-opus-5` | 16,850 | 39,946 | 225 | 0.194118 | 1.00 |
| `opus`, `high` | `claude-opus-5-5` | 15,878 | 38,989 | 202 | 0.138878 | **0.72** |
| `opus`, `medium` | `claude-opus-5-5` | 14,212 | 40,656 | 225 | 0.126343 | 0.65 |

- Recomputing each row from the price table reproduces `total_cost_usd`, with the 1 hour cache write price, as in note 02.
- Opus 5 billed $0.1941 here against $0.1836 on 2.1.274, because about 6% more prompt was written to the cache. The ratio between versions carries over. The absolute figures do not.
- The `medium` launch followed the `high` launch on the same model, so part of its prompt was already cached: fewer writes and more reads. Its 0.65 overstates what `medium` saves over `high`. On this shape effort barely moves the bill, since output is about 3% of it.

By effort, where the card states it in its text:

- **CursorBench 4.0** (section 8.8, measured by Cursor). Opus 5.5 scores 57.8% at `max`, 56.0% at `xhigh` and at `high`, and 52.5% at `medium`. Fable 5.1 scores 51.8% at `max`, for $17.28 a problem, and Opus 5 scores 46.6% at `max`, for $11.95. Anthropic estimates Opus 5.5 at about $4 a problem at `high` and about $3 at `medium`.
- **FrontierCode** (section 8.4). Opus 5.5 peaks at `medium`, with 54.6% on Main and 65.3% on Extended. "Scores decline above medium effort but mostly recover at max", because the grading penalizes out-of-scope changes. Decision 16's cap cites the same effect for Opus 5 and Fable 5.1 above `high`. For Opus 5.5 it starts one step lower.
- **Terminal-Bench 4.0** (section 8.5). Opus 5.5 scores 66.4% at `xhigh` and 64.8% at `max`, within noise of each other.

**Honesty** (section 6.5.4) is the axis the M5 writer finding touches. On AA-Omniscience, Opus 5.5 has the best factual-accuracy net score of the Claude models (0.58), within error bars of the two Mythos versions. It also discloses concealed git changes more often than its predecessors (96.9%). On MASK, honesty under pressure, it scores below Opus 5 and Sonnet 5 and above Mythos 5.1. Nothing in the card measures a writer's claims against the evidence it cites, which is what the M5 verifier failed.

The announcement adds two points. "At default settings it will cost 40% less than Opus 5 on typical workloads." And subscribers get higher five-hour limits and a rate limit reset "which you can now save and use whenever you choose".

## 4. What it means for wayline

- **Nothing breaks.** The launcher's check, the verifier table keyed by family, and the Opus roles take Opus 5.5 as they took Opus 5. The M5 verifier's sample already ran on Opus 5.5 (`docs/verification/m5.md`). `RESOLVED_MODELS` in `wayline/worker.py` still tells the fake launcher that `opus` reports `claude-opus-5`. That is stale and harmless.
- **The build grades.** `models.md` put 50 and 53 on Fable because each writes role prompts that every later project run inherits. That reason now points to Opus 5.5, at about a third of Fable's cost and outside Fable's weekly window.
- **The rung ladder and the roles.** Rung 5 (`opus high`) now outranks rungs 6 and 7 at about a third of their cost. A tier up from rung 5 therefore buys a dearer, weaker model, and difficulties 4 and 5 start there. The planner (`fable xhigh`), the decider (`fable max`), the writer (`fable xhigh`), the evolver (`fable xhigh`), and the verifier of an Opus author (`fable high`) all sit on Fable. The M5 prose the verifier failed came from the writer on `fable xhigh`. Changing any of this amends decisions 14 and 16. Decision 14's policy table is test-checked against `policy.json` (`tests/spec_table.py`), so the ticket that changes `policy.json` edits that table in the same commit.
- **Open for that amendment:**
  - What difficulties 4 and 5 map to once no Fable rung sits above rung 5.
  - Whether `medium` belongs on the rung ladder, since Opus 5.5 peaks there on FrontierCode.
  - Whether the verifier of an Opus author stays on another family. That is a choice about independence, not a benchmark question, and the gates already accept a verifier on the author's model under the fallback.
- **Not measured here:** Opus 5.5 at `low`, which is rung 4. It has neither a probe nor a catalog estimate.

## Caveats

- **One launch per row.** Each configuration was probed once, on one shape and one day. The ratios come from the price table, because the token counts match, so they hold for any shape where the two versions bill alike.
- **Whose benchmarks.** The figures are Anthropic's or its partners', mostly at max effort. Cross-card comparisons carry about five points of slack (note 03).
