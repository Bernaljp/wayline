# Protocol

1. Read the claim, the kind and register, and the target the prompt gives.
2. Open every data file listed, enough to know what the drafts may and may not show.
3. Open every draft's PNG. Open its script when you need to know what it plotted.
4. Score every draft on the four criteria, 0 to 5 each, and rank them all, 1 first, with no ties;
   a draft never outranks one whose four scores add up to more.
5. Write the reasons from what you saw, criterion by criterion; they are what a person reads when
   they take a rank other than 1.
6. Return the ranking once, through the structured output, before you stop. You write no file.
